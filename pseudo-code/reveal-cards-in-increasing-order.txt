Reveal Cards In Increasing Order

You are given an integer array deck. There is a deck of cards 
where every card has a unique integer. The integer on 
the ith card is deck[i].

You can order the deck in any order you want. 
Initially, all the cards start face down (unrevealed) 
in one deck.

You will do the following steps repeatedly until all 
cards are revealed:

Take the top card of the deck, reveal it, and take it out of 
the deck.
If there are still cards in the deck then put the next top 
card of the deck at the bottom of the deck.
If there are still unrevealed cards, go back to step 1. 
Otherwise, stop.
Return an ordering of the deck that would reveal the cards 
in increasing order.

Note that the first entry in the answer is considered to be 
the top of the deck.

In order to solve this problem, we follow the algorithm given,

i = 0
order_of_cards = []
simul_deck = [0, 1, 2...]
while simul_deck:
    curr = deck.popleft()
    to_move = deck.popleft
    deck.append(to_move)

    order_of_cards.append(curr)

now we will have the order_of_cards, which shows
from where each card came.
Then we just have to order the deck, and then fill
the deck based on the order_of_cards
